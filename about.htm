About Hewitt Storage

Secure container storage at Hewitt Business Park – on simple monthly terms.

  • Monitored site with private access
  • Open 24/7
  • Monthly terms – no long contracts
  • Close proximity to M6 / M58

Hewitt Storage provides secure steel container storage at Hewitt Business Park, a family run, four acre site on Winstanley Road, approximately half a mile from Winstanley College. The M6 / M58 runs just behind the site, and Orrell train station and bus links are both within 200 metres.

Our 20ft × 8ft × 8ft steel containers sit in a fenced compound with lighting and motorised gates. The site is surrounded by steel palisade fencing with a single vehicle access via fob-controlled security gates, and the site has 24/7 remote monitoring. You get your own private key fob, so you can come and go at any time.

We make storage as simple as possible: flexible monthly terms with a minimum rental period of 3 months, no set-up fees, and rent collected by Direct Debit. We hold one month’s rent as a security bond, refundable less any outstanding charges at the end of your rental.

Containers are let to both businesses and individuals – for stock, tools and equipment, household contents during a move, or anything that needs a secure, dry home. You’ll need your own insurance for the contents.
